About

Fresno's Premier
Haunted Attraction

Fresno Fright Nights is a new immersive haunted attraction in the heart of the San Joaquin Valley. Built to push fears to the limit and tell stories that hit different, FFN brings Hollywood-level scares to Central California.

Six acres. Multiple haunted zones. Eerie passageways, spine-chilling encounters, and a midway packed with food, drinks, and chaos. This is not a walk-through haunted house. This is a destination.

What Awaits

The Haunts

Four distinct worlds of dread. Each one built to break you in a different way.

☢
[HAUNT PHOTO: CHEMIKILL]
Walking Haunt - 1/4 Mile

ChemiKill

A toxic spill at Chemi-Kill Industries left the workforce... changed. Mutated workers now roam the ruins, dripping with radioactive sludge. Officials have sent civilians in unarmed to gauge the horrors' response. That's you. The quarter-mile walk out is the longest of your life.

🍕
[HAUNT PHOTO: NIGHT CRAWLERS PIZZA]
Indoor Haunt - Animatronic

Night Crawlers Pizza

Once a cryptid-themed family spot, black magic gave its animatronics life. Now haunted and abandoned, guests have gone missing. Rumors swirl of a cult of obsessed kids and creatures that still move in the dark - hungry to finish what Lars Durney started.

🚣
[HAUNT PHOTO: BLOOD RIVER BAYOU]
Raft Ride - Lazy River

Blood River Bayou

The Hoodoo Hag rules this swamp. A vengeful sorceress commanding undead horrors, her ghostly Mardi Gras lures victims every season. Ride the river barge. Face her monsters. The Lantern Keeper has returned to challenge her - but will it be enough to end the curse before it ends you?

⚰
[HAUNT PHOTO: GHOUL TOWN TRAILS]
Outdoor Walking Haunt

Ghoul Town Trails

Beneath the city lies a forgotten graveyard where the dead walk in silence. A cursed bell awakened ghouls and cultists who now guard the only path through. You once knew this land as the miniature golf course. The dead repossessed it. Every step echoes with the bell's call.

General admission includes access to all four haunted zones plus the full Midway experience:
  • ChemiKill - the quarter-mile walk through an abandoned chemical plant
  • Night Crawlers Pizza - indoor animatronic cryptid haunt
  • Blood River Bayou - raft ride on the lazy river
  • Ghoul Town Trails - outdoor cemetery walking haunt
