Park Rules & Guest Information

Fresno, California

🍔
Food & Outside Items ▼
  • Food or drinks may be brought into the park.
  • No glass containers or kitchen knives are allowed.
  • The use of grills or any open flame on park property, including the parking lot, is prohibited.
  • No cooking or warming food of any kind.
  • BBQ Cove Cabanas include a designated grilling area. The only exception for grills. (Cabana reservation required.)
Bag Inspections
  • For guest safety and to ensure proper items are being brought inside the park, our staff at the main gate may inspect ice chests, bags, totes, and purses.
🏊
Water Safety ▼
  • Life vests are available and recommended for all guests.
  • Guests under 48" (4 feet) are encouraged to wear a life vest.
  • In our Wave Pool, guests under 48" require a life vest and be accompanied by an adult within arms reach.
  • Diving is not allowed in any pool or attraction.
Flotation Devices
  • No personal flotation devices may be brought into the park.
  • For infants and toddlers, only foam-based flotation seats are permitted. Devices may not include lap straps or restraining strap. They may be used ONLY in the Lazy River.
  • Personal life vests are permitted, but MUST be U.S. Coast Guard Approved.
  • Non-swimmers are always encouraged to wear a life vest.
🚫
Park Conduct ▼
  • No running.
  • No loitering, unruly behavior, or line cutting.
  • No amplified audio or TVs are allowed in the park.
  • Guests must refrain from using foul, profane offensive language or gestures.
  • Guests must abide by posted attraction instructions, lifeguard & management instructions.
🚭
Smoking & Shade ▼
Shade Structures
  • Personal shade structures are permitted only within our six designated areas.
  • Shade structures must not obstruct any paths or walkways or other guests' personal space.
Smoke-Free Park
  • Island Waterpark is a smoke-free park.
  • Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is only permitted in designated areas.
👙
Swimwear Policy ▼
  • Proper swimwear must be worn on all rides and attractions. Examples: Dry fit, and spandex material. No cotton.
  • Inappropriate swimwear is not permitted. See below.
  • Any child requiring diapers must wear a waterproof swim diaper with swimwear bottoms over it, prior to entering the water. Swim diapers are available for purchase at the Gift Shop.
Inappropriate Swimwear & Clothing (Not Permitted)
  • Underwear, boxers, briefs
  • Clothing that is transparent or translucent in private areas
  • Attire with exposed metal, rivets, buttons, zippers, buckles, or snaps
  • Long pants, jeans, denim, slacks, or trousers
  • Any clothing or accessory (e.g. belts, or jewelry)
  • Clothing that offensively exposes parts of the body
  • Indecent pictures, slogans or graphics
  • Clothing or tattoos with offensive language, graphics or symbols
📏
Height & Weight Guidelines ▼
  • Height and weight restrictions are posted for all attractions and enforced.
🔒
Park Property & Safety ▼
  • No alcohol or illegal drugs may be brought into the park.
  • Island Waterpark is private property. We do not allow firearms, weapons, glass containers, alcohol, radios/boom boxes, musical instruments, and unauthorized flotation devices.
  • Medical Marijuana is prohibited in the park or in the parking lot.
📷
Photos & Recordings ▼
  • Photos or recording are strictly prohibited in restrooms, changing rooms and locker areas.
  • Island Waterpark management reserves the right to stop picture taking or video recording and confiscate any appropriate material.
🚑
Injuries & Emergencies ▼
  • Any injuries sustained at the park must be reported the day of the incident.
  • If you need help due to injury or emergency, immediately notify any employee or visit our First Aid Station for any concerns.
⚠️

Failure to follow park rules may result in ejection without a refund. Island Waterpark management reserves the right to refuse admission or remove guests who violate park policies. The policies are in place for the safety and enjoyment of all guests. Policies are subject to change without notice.
