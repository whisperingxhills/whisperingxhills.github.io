Official Mobile App Proposal
The dead don't rest. Neither should the experience.
Prepared for Season 2 — 2026
The Problem
Every haunted attraction in the country has the same guest friction points.
⏳
Blind Wait Times
Guests don't know which haunt has the shortest line until they walk to it. This creates bottlenecks at popular attractions and dead zones at others.
🗺️
Navigation Confusion
6 acres in the dark. First time visitors don't know where anything is. They miss attractions, miss food, miss merch, and leave early.
📱
No Digital Touchpoint
Once a guest buys a ticket, there's no ongoing relationship until next year. No engagement between visits, no personalization, no data.
📸
Missed Shareability
Guests take photos, post stories, tag the location. But there's no branded experience they share that drives new visitors directly to tickets.
The Solution
A dedicated Fresno Fright Nights mobile app that extends the haunt beyond the physical gates.
🗺️
Interactive Dark Map
Real time map of the entire 6 acre property. Haunts, food trucks, bar, restrooms, photo ops, merch. GPS enabled with an atmosphere that matches the event.
⏱️
Live Wait Times
Estimated wait time per haunt, updated in real time. Guests plan their route intelligently. Even distribution across attractions means better throughput for operations.
🎟️
Tickets in App
Purchase, upgrade, and manage tickets directly. Digital ticket display on arrival. Upsell opportunities for VIP, Frightening Lane, and add-ons throughout the night.
📖
Lore Codex
Each haunt has deep backstory. The app unlocks lore entries as guests complete attractions, turning a night out into a narrative experience they want to finish.
🏆
Achievements & Badges
Shareable badges for completing all 4 haunts, visiting on Halloween, going solo, hitting VIP. Gamification drives repeat visits and social sharing.
😱
Scare Tracker
Rate your fear level after each haunt. See aggregate scare scores across all guests. Social feature that creates conversation and competition.

Guest Journey
The app touches every stage of the experience.
1
Before They Arrive
Days / weeks before visit
Guest downloads app, browses haunts, reads lore teasers, purchases tickets. Push notifications for special event nights and flash deals. Anticipation builds before they ever step on property.
2
At the Gate
Arrival
Digital ticket scan. Interactive map loads with their current location. They immediately know where every haunt, food truck, and bar is. No confusion, no wasted time.
3
During the Night
On property
Check wait times before committing to a line. Track which haunts they've completed. Unlock lore entries and achievements as they progress. Rate scares. Find food when hungry.
4
After They Leave
Post visit
Share achievements and scare scores on social media. Browse unfinished lore entries (reason to come back). Receive push notifications for remaining event nights. Refer friends.
5
Next Season
Year round
App stays installed. Pre-season teaser content drops. Early access tickets for returning app users. The relationship doesn't end on November 1st.
Operational Benefits
The app doesn't just improve the guest experience. It improves operations.
  • Crowd distribution — live wait times naturally spread guests across haunts instead of everyone queuing at the same one. Better throughput, shorter actual waits, happier guests.
  • In-app upsell — guest is in a 30 minute line. Push notification: "Skip the line? Upgrade to Frightening Lane for $20 more." Contextual upselling at the moment of highest motivation.
  • Data collection — which haunts are most popular, peak hours, dwell time, food and drink patterns, ticket tier preferences. Data driven decisions for Season 3 and beyond.
  • Reduced staffing pressure — fewer guests asking "where is X?" when the map is in their pocket. Information booth traffic drops. Staff focus on experience, not directions.
  • Direct marketing channel — push notifications replace paid social ads for reaching existing guests. Free re-engagement for every future event.
  • Sponsor integration — in-app sponsor placements (Liquid Death, local businesses) create a new revenue stream without physical signage.
