The Concept

Containment. No Way Out.

The Setting

An abandoned mall or large retail space - 15,000 to 30,000 sq ft of controllable, camera-ready environment. Multiple distinct zones. Full power infrastructure. Climate controlled. We will help source and secure the venue using our network of production contacts and location experience.


The Theme

Zombie apocalypse. The contestants are locked inside an abandoned facility as the outbreak spreads. The space transforms around them - walls break, explosions erupt, creatures appear, power goes out. Nothing is safe. Nothing is what it seems.


The Twist

Every "disaster" is a precisely engineered, remotely triggered special effect - controlled by production in real time. The contestants don't know what's real and what isn't. The audience does.

Why Indoor > Outdoor

Camera Infrastructure - Hardwired power + networking. No battery swaps. No signal drops. 24/7 coverage.

SFX Control - Every effect is pre-rigged, remotely triggered, show-controlled, and repeatable. No weather dependency.

Set Design - Build once, dress multiple zones. Retail spaces have open floor plans, high ceilings, existing electrical.

Viewer Experience - Better lighting = better cameras = better content. Controlled atmospherics on demand.

Safety - Controlled environment. No wildlife, no exposure, no terrain hazards. Every SFX is crowd-rated and haunt-tested.

Effects Showcase

What We Bring to the Screen

Every effect below is crowd-safe, remotely triggered, show-controlled, and remotely triggered - designed for live audiences in haunted attractions. Zero pyrotechnics. Zero danger. Maximum impact.

Car Crash

A pneumatic ram pushes a real car front-clip through a breakaway wall. Headlights flash, horn blares, speakers deliver a massive crash sound through a sub. Contestants think a car just drove through the building.

Est. Cost: ~$2,500

Dumpster Explosion

Geyser fog machine fires a 15ft column of vapor, synced to a strobe flash, sub-bass rumble, and scattered lightweight debris. Reads as an explosion on camera and in person.

Est. Cost: ~$2,600

Power Outage

DMX controller kills all set lighting simultaneously. Emergency lights flicker on. Sirens fade in. Total environmental control - production decides when the lights come back.

Est. Cost: ~$1,600

Creature Reveal

Fog burst + strobe + directional spot + audio sting. A zombie appears from nowhere, fully lit for 2 seconds, then vanishes into blackout. Simple rig, massive impact.

Est. Cost: ~$1,000

Atmospheric Zones

Full environmental dressing per zone - haze, spatial audio, accent lighting, scenic paint. Each zone feels like a different part of the outbreak. Controllable mood on demand.

Est. Cost: ~$1,000/zone

Monster Costumes

Professional SFX makeup + foam prosthetics + distressed wardrobe. Haunted house grade - designed to look real under direct lighting and on camera.

Est. Cost: <$250/character
