Welcome to the Cabin Map

Step 1: Use the date picker in the sidebar to choose your night. Cabin pins are color-coded by vibe. Available cabins are bright and clickable, booked cabins appear dimmed.

Step 2: Browse the map to find a cabin you like. Drag to move around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Step 3: Tap any cabin pin to see details: capacity, theming, and what your rental includes.

Step 4: Click the "Book Now" button to reserve your cabin. You'll be taken to a secure checkout.

Tip: Shipwreck VIP Cabins are evening only, for the night you select. Fresno Fright Nights operates 11 nights in October.
