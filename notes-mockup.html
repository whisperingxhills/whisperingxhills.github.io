📋 Mix Checklist
R&B / Pop Template
PRE-MIX SETUP
✓ Organize and color code all tracks
✓ Label everything (no "Audio 1" or "Track 14")
✓ Set up bus routing (vox, drums, music, fx)
✓ Gain stage every track to ~-18dBFS
✓ Load reference track, level match
LOW END
✓ High pass everything that isn't kick or 808
✓ Check kick and 808 relationship (sidechain or pattern)
○ Solo bass in mono, make sure it translates
○ A/B low end against reference
VOCALS
✓ Edit breaths (reduce, don't remove)
✓ Pitch correction (transparent, not robotic unless intentional)
○ De-ess before compression
○ Compress in stages (2-3dB per compressor, stack light)
○ EQ: cut mud 200-400hz, presence 2-5kHz, air 10kHz+
○ Vocal verb and delay (send, not insert)
○ Ad libs panned and tucked, doubles slightly wider
○ Check vocal sits on top of beat at every section
DRUMS & PERCUSSION
○ Snare / clap: crack and body balanced, not boxy
○ Hi hats: tame harsh frequencies above 10kHz
○ Percs panned for width (shakers, rims, snaps)
○ Drum bus compression (glue, 2-4dB GR)
INSTRUMENTS & SYNTHS
○ Keys / pads: carve space for vocals (cut 1-4kHz)
○ Guitars: check stereo spread, mono compat
○ Synths: automate movement (filter, volume, pan)
○ Nothing fighting the vocal for center stage
MIX BUS & FINAL
○ Check mono compatibility (whole mix)
○ Listen at low volume: can you hear vocal + kick + snare?
○ Listen on phone speaker, car, earbuds
○ Automation passes (vocal rides, mutes, builds)
○ LUFS check (target -14 for streaming)
○ Leave 3-6dB headroom for mastering
○ Bounce and listen fresh tomorrow before sending
